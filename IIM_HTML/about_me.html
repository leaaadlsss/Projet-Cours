Some little explanations

The prices I set can seem quite high for many since I'm a student but I make art on my free time and I can't do all of it at my own expense.

You can't ask me to do a painting or animation with a deadline of a week, I can do two minimum if the project isn't too demanding. And yes, I know having to wait can be unbearable for some people. But I pour my own tears, sweat and soul into this so you better be patient.
